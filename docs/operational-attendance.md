# Phase 4 operational attendance

## Local verification

The operational station requires `SCANNER_OPERATIONAL_SYNC=true` and Laravel's `local` or `testing` environment. The default is false. Use an isolated application, a newly created disposable database, localhost or HTTPS, synthetic accounts and a dedicated browser profile. Verify the effective database, cache and session configuration before migrating. SQLite supports the repository's full migration chain. See the MySQL installation finding below before attempting a fresh MySQL installation.

1. Create a synthetic scanner operator with scanner operation permission, an active terminal, an eligible student with a QR card and an assigned class. Select the terminal at `/attendance-scanner`. A scanner-security administrator can confirm their password and pair the browser. Rehearsal and operational modes share only device pairing information. The station has its own IndexedDB queue and lease history.
2. Open the station while connected. Check the status and disconnect the browser. Scan within the class window. Check that “QR Scan Captured” appears after the IndexedDB commit, and that the pending count increases. Try two rapid scans. Reconnect in the same tab and check the two individual decisions and the original times. Repeat a request after losing its response. Compare the UUID, receipt, audit reference and canonical attendance count.
3. With a synthetic 11:00–12:00 class, capture at 11:05 and synchronize at 11:30. Expect Present. Repeat with separate fixtures at the grace boundary and just after it. An existing system absence can become Present or Late. Inspect the receipt’s before and after images and linked audit and attendance rows. Create a manual correction, change enrollment, move a timetable or rotate the credential during an interruption and verify the review outcome.
4. Run the application's existing minute scheduler against the disposable database. Wait longer than the 90-second threshold. Inspect the interval start and occurrence holds. Confirm that ordinary attendance remains visible and affected automatic absences are omitted from summaries, PDFs and warnings. Restore network access and check the signed checkpoint, contiguous receipts and subsequent scheduler result. Repeat with a missing sequence and with a rejected event. A heartbeat and an empty client queue are insufficient to release a hold.
5. Inspect `GET /scanner-security/offline-sessions/{uuid}?resource=receipts` as a password-confirmed scanner-security administrator. Other supported resources are `interruptions`, `obligations` and `conflicts`. The read-only endpoint paginates records. Follow attendance and audit IDs for investigation. Browser restart during a disconnection can invalidate monotonic clock evidence and lead to review. After an expired capture lease, confirm the operator password again to upload.
6. Exercise personnel windows with a synthetic instructor or staff account. Verify the four stages in order, cooldown restrictions, a conflicting later scan, approved leave, a weekend and a school-wide non-working day. The existing scanner and work-calendar services handle those exclusions differently. Operational reconciliation sends a calendar disagreement to review.

## Attendance and coverage records

Each committed scan has a UUID, capture time, local order and per-lease sequence. Batches of at most 25 are signed using the paired device key and a fresh, mode-bound challenge. The server checks the selected terminal, operator, device, signed bytes, original authorization window, clock evidence, sequence and previous receipts. It reserves the UUID, uses the existing attendance service at the validated instant, records the audit reference and commits the decision together. An identical retry retrieves the existing receipt. Conflicting payloads produce conflict evidence. Rehearsal receipts and TEST- cards cannot create canonical records.

The transport can receive an event before its evaluation completes in the same transaction. Its terminal decisions are confirmed, duplicate, rejected and review_required. Only a confirmed or existing canonical positive represents attendance. Operational receipts store a keyed credential fingerprint rather than the QR itself. Pending and review records on the browser can still contain the card value. Confirmed and duplicate receipts remove the raw card from the local record.

At lease creation the server records an eligibility revision, a policy fingerprint and potentially affected classes, events and personnel periods. Transactional change tracking covers identity, credential, roster, approval, enrollment, timetable, instructor, leave, cancellation and school-event inputs. The original occurrence resolver and effective dates still apply. Person-specific changes can be distinguished from shared changes. A changed shared input can require review across several occurrences. The server cannot reconstruct unrecorded historical states. Before changing attendance configuration or deploying different rule logic during a local trial, run `php artisan scanner:invalidate-attendance-policy` to invalidate outstanding evidence, including changes subsequently reverted.

The station normally sends a heartbeat every 30 seconds and can capture for eight hours from lease issue. A signed checkpoint binds a persisted watermark to a server challenge. The scheduler measures a coverage gap from the last proven instant, including time before suspected disconnection. It escalates after one operating day, skipping weekends and published school-wide closures. Negative outcomes wait for coverage through the affected scan window or lease expiry. This may delay absence presentation beyond the ordinary grace cutoff. The original absence writer also checks the hold when called directly after cancellation rejection.

Full coverage requires contiguous durable receipts and resolved outcomes. A timestamp-validated business rejection can keep its overlapping occurrences provisional while independent windows reconcile. Gaps, ambiguous clocks or changed shared eligibility can require broader review within an affected lease. Reconciliation locks each obligation, applies the original class or event rules and invalidates reporting caches after commit. Personnel reporting uses the same holds when inferring absences from missing logs. The backend leaves unresolved decisions available for authorized review. Phase 5 owns monitoring and case disposition.

## Trial limitations

The Phase 4 trial assumes a dedicated station browser profile and a trustworthy application origin. Device signatures establish possession of the paired key. Physical presence, device clock accuracy after a restart, survival of browser storage eviction and actions taken outside recorded database history still need independent evidence. A lost device or unresolved clock dispute can leave the affected lease under review. Keep the trial configuration active while examining its provisional reporting state. Switching the flag off restores legacy reporting.

## MySQL installation finding

On clean MySQL 8.4.3, the November 6 QR migration fails when it adds the STORED generated column `active_user_id`. Its expression references `qr_credentials.user_id`, whose foreign key `qr_credentials_user_id_foreign` specifies ON UPDATE CASCADE and ON DELETE RESTRICT. MySQL restricts cascading updates on a base column used by a stored generated column. Earlier DDL in the migration can already have added `revocation_reason`. [MySQL generated-column restrictions](https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.4/en/create-table-generated-columns.html)

The repaired November 6 migration uses an indexed VIRTUAL column and recognizes a previously added revocation reason. A November 25 forward migration verifies existing installations and rebuilds the original STORED definition when present. Both use the same schema and duplicate-data preflight. Before deployment, verify the hosting engine/version, ALTER and index privileges, trigger privileges for the full chain, and sufficient disk space for a table rebuild. Restore a backup into an isolated database first. Pause credential writes and user ID changes during migration and validation. Unexpected definitions or duplicate active credentials require investigation. Leave the repaired constraint in place for application rollback. Schema rollback requires a verified backup because the original rollback deletes revocation reasons.

## Repeating the automated checks

Run `php vendor/bin/phpunit --filter 'OperationalAttendanceTest|operational_mode_binds' --do-not-cache-result` for historical decisions, interruptions, reconciliation and mode isolation. Run `node --test tests/js/*.test.js` and `node --test tests/browser/scanner-preview.mjs tests/browser/scanner-rehearsal.mjs` for client regressions and Chrome tests with temporary profiles.

The MySQL race test requires a fresh disposable database named `iqams_phase4_verification` on loopback port 13329, `APP_ENV=testing`, a separate configuration cache, empty DB_URL, and a migrated schema. Run `php vendor/bin/phpunit tests/Integration/OperationalAttendanceRaceTest.php --do-not-cache-result`. It starts four independent PHP processes and retains the synthetic fixture for inspection. Use a fresh disposable database for a repeat.

Known scanner response failures predating Phase 4 are the missing third student detail label (“Year Level”) and third instructor detail value (“Instructor”) in QrAttendanceScannerTest. Their assertions were preserved.

## Verification record

Both headless Chrome scenarios passed. One verified queue commits, retry and survival across offline reload and browser restart. The paired Laravel scenario covered a lost response, two rehearsal tabs, an operational student scan captured during network loss and confirmed after reconnection, and a restarted rehearsal capture sent to clock review. The tests used disposable browser profiles and synthetic data. A physical scanner, computer power cycle and hosted cPanel installation were outside this verification.

The focused Phase 4 suite passed 27 tests and 127 assertions. The independent MySQL race passed 12 assertions, including identical retry receipts, one attendance record at the original capture time, one audit entry and a reconciled class obligation. All 19 JavaScript unit tests passed. The daily personnel report suite passed separately with 14 tests and 1,089 assertions.

The wider PHPUnit run excluding that report suite completed 420 tests with 402 passes, 17 assertion failures and one error. The two scanner profile assertions above account for two failures. The other failures were in account invitation (1), dashboard performance (4), ID card payloads (2), instructor class attendance UI (2), profile access (1), office units (1), QR administration (1), staff attendance pages (1), staff dashboard (1) and staff profile layout (1). The error was an undefined `instructor.history` route. Their origin was not independently established against the original dirty tree. The focused run includes two additional tests for a school closure and a declared policy change added after the wider run started.
